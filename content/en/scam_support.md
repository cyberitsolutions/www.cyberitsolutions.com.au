If you've been targeted by a scam, you’re not alone. An increasing number of Australians are becoming victims of scams, often originating from malicious email, deceptive telephone calls, vulnerable computer software, and so on. Taking proactive steps is the best way to prevent further risks. Our guide, [Personal Cyber Safety Guide for Australian Technology Users](/Online%20Security%20and%20Safety%20Guidelines%20-%20Cyber%20IT%20Solutions.pdf), provides clear, step-by-step advice to secure your accounts, recognize future threats, and regain peace of mind. Developed by Australian cybersecurity experts, this guide covers:

✅ Immediate actions to lock down compromised data.

✅ Long-term strategies to block scammers (emails, calls, phishing).

✅ Red flags and tools to stay ahead of evolving tactics.
